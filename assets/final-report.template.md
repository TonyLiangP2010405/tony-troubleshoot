# WyreStorm diagnostic report

## Outcome

Status: **incomplete until coverage audit passes**

## Scope and collection window

Record timezone, start/end timestamps, endpoints, in-scope models/firmware, document IDs and hashes, and redaction policy.

## Online device inventory

Summarize devices and link each item to discovery evidence.

## Findings

Summarize confirmed facts first, then interpretations, with raw evidence paths.

## Coverage proof

Report command, parameter-row, device, page/cursor, response-field, completion, failure, unsupported, and blocked counts. Record the two final discovery passes.

## Unresolved gaps

List every unavailable item. If this section is non-empty, keep the overall status partial/incomplete.

## Resume/audit instructions

Point to `session-handoff.md`, `checkpoint.json`, ledgers, and raw evidence.
